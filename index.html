💡 How Smart Irrigation Works

1️⃣ Soil moisture sensor checks the soil.

2️⃣ If the soil becomes dry, the pump turns ON.

3️⃣ When enough moisture is available, the pump turns OFF.

4️⃣ This helps reduce unnecessary water usage.
